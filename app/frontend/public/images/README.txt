STORE PHOTOS
============
Drop the real store photographs into this folder with these exact names:

  store-1.jpg   (large photo — storefront or main view)
  store-2.jpg   (inside the store / products)
  store-3.jpg   (inside the store / products)

They will automatically appear in the "About Us" section of the website.
JPG or JPEG format works best. Keep each photo under ~500 KB for fast loading.

LOGO
====
To use the real M K & B logo, replace the file:
  /app/frontend/public/logo.svg
with your logo file (keep the name logo.svg, or save a PNG as logo.png and
update the two <img src="/logo.svg"> references in src/components/Navbar.jsx,
Hero.jsx and Footer.jsx).
